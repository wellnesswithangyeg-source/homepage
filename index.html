A little about me

Wellness that fits a real, full life

I split my days between two worlds that turn out to have more in common than you'd think: leading commercial strategy for a facility management company, and coaching people through the much slower, quieter work of eating well and moving with intention.

Both come down to the same thing — paying close attention, cutting the noise, and being honest about what actually works. On this side of my life, that means recipes built around what's genuinely in a Cairo kitchen, and guidance that always tells you the one thing to watch, not just the upside.
